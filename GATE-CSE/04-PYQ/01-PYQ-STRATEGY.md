# GATE CS — PYQ Strategy

## Core Philosophy
PYQs are the curriculum map. The goal is to understand **why** an answer is correct, not memorize it.

## Topic-Wise vs Year-Wise
- Phases 1–5: topic-wise PYQs immediately after completing each topic.
- Phase 6: year-wise full-paper simulation under strict 3-hour conditions.

## Years to Solve
- 2019–2020: pattern familiarization.
- 2021–2026: mandatory core window in the source guide.
- 2015–2018: targeted supplementation for graph algorithms, TOC, COA and similar areas.
- Pre-2015: lower priority because of syllabus/pattern drift.

## Tiered Repetition
- Tier 1: 2–3 passes.
- Tier 2: 2 passes.
- Tier 3–4: at least 1 complete pass.
- Wrong answers: revisit after 48 hours, 1 week and 1 month.

## Timing
Solve topic PYQs within 24–48 hours of study. Begin full-paper simulation after the early study phases; maintain approximately one full paper every two weeks from the integration stage.

## Critical Sets
Graph Algorithms 2015–2026; CPU Scheduling 2018–2026; Subnetting 2017–2026; TOC 2016–2026; SQL 2018–2026; COA Cache 2017–2026.

## Mistakes to Avoid
Do not solve before learning the topic; do not skip detailed solutions; do not spend equal time on old/outdated papers; do not solve in answer-check mode; do not ignore MSQs.
