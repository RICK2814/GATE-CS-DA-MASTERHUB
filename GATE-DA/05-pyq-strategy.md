# GATE DA — PYQ Strategy

## Core Philosophy
Use PYQs to test transfer and pattern recognition, not to memorize answers.

## Topic-Wise vs Year-Wise
- First pass: topic-wise PYQs after concept completion.
- Second pass: year-wise full papers under timed conditions.
- Final pass: wrong, guessed and slow questions.

## Historical Window
DA started in 2024; the primary source window is 2024–2026.

## Repetition Loop
Solve → inspect full reasoning → classify mistake → revisit concept → re-solve after 24–48 hours.

## Full-Paper Integration
Use full papers after each major phase and track time, accuracy and question-type behavior.
