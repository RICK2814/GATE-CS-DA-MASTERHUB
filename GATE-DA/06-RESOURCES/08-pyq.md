## PYQ Workflow

### Pass 1 — Topic-Wise
Finish a topic, then solve its relevant PYQs immediately.

### Pass 2 — Mixed
Combine related topics to remove topic clues.

### Pass 3 — Year-Wise
Attempt complete papers under timed conditions.

### Pass 4 — Error-Only
Create a separate pool of:
- wrong questions
- guessed questions
- slow questions
- questions missed because of misreading

### Pass 5 — Re-Solve
Return to the error pool after a gap and solve without looking at the original solution.
