# GATE DA — Subject-wise Tier Analysis

## Structural Foundation
65 questions, 100 marks, 3 hours. General Aptitude is fixed at 15 marks. The DA paper started in 2024, so the historical window is 2024–2026.

## Critical 2026 Trend Alerts
- Probability & Statistics: 10 → 12 → 13 questions (2024 → 2025 → 2026).
- Database Management & Warehousing: 4 → 7 → 11 questions.
- Calculus & Optimization: 5 → 6 → 2 questions.
- Programming, DSA & Algorithms: 13 → 9 → 9.
- Machine Learning remains consistently important.

## Tier Structure
### TIER 1
1. Probability & Statistics — distributions, Bayes, hypothesis testing, CLT, expectation/variance, covariance.
2. Machine Learning — regression, classification, trees, bias/variance, regularization, clustering, PCA, evaluation.
3. Database Management & Warehousing — SQL, normalization, ER, indexing, warehousing/OLAP.
4. Programming, Data Structures & Algorithms — Python, DS, sorting, graph algorithms.

### TIER 2
5. Linear Algebra — vectors, matrices, rank/nullity, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, orthogonality, SVD.
6. Artificial Intelligence — logic, search, adversarial search, probabilistic reasoning, inference.

### TIER 3
7. Calculus & Optimization — limits, derivatives, maxima-minima, Taylor series, partial differentiation, optimization.
